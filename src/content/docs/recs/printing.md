## Why?

When you're marketing a product to anyone that's not the precise end user, you need to drive adoption. One thing that really can keep you top of mind is if there's an unskippable, unmissable piece of physical paper in front of someone.

There are a lot of different options on the internet in terms of where you can print, but the below are my suggestions.

> Pro Tip: All of these places will send you a sample of their paper stocks / selection for free. Take them up on it.

### Online

1. Moo
1. Vistaprint

### New York

1. [Printmor](https://printmor.com/)
    * Classic NYC instituion with good prices, high touch, and same-day / rush printing.

### Avoid

1. Staples
1. FedEx
1. UPS
